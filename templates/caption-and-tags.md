# The caption + tags (the part that travels)

The image is only half the post. The caption and the tags are what push it into other people's feeds. This is the flywheel, so don't skip it.

## Caption (the text field, not the words on the image)
Keep it short and human. Two or three lines.
- What happened, concrete: "45 of us built together in Austin last night."
- One specific detail or a small human moment: "Six projects went live. One person shipped their first ever."
- A soft invite: "We run these every month. Come to the next one."

Write like you'd text a friend who wasn't there, not a press release.

## Tags (the reach engine)
- **Tag the people in the photos.** This is the single biggest lever. Each person you tag puts the post in front of their followers.
- **Tag @NextWork** so we can reshare and amplify it.
- Tag the venue (cafe, space) if they hosted you. They often reshare too.

Why it works: the platforms now weight shares and reach to new audiences heavily, and a post from you (an actual person) travels further than the same thing reshared from a brand account.

## Hashtags
A few, not twenty. One broad plus a couple specific:
`#buildandbrew #nextwork #<yourcity>tech`

## Credit + the on-ramp (the loop, in your voice)
This is where a viewer becomes the next builder. Do it like a person, not a brand. The post is about
you and your room first; the credit and the invite are a P.S., not the pitch. One line is plenty.

**Credit NextWork the way you'd credit anything that got you here.** Honest, specific, yours:
- "Wild that I ran this. A year ago I couldn't build. NextWork changed that."
- "Everything I know about shipping I learned at nextwork.ai."
- "This is what a few months of building at NextWork looks like."

**Leave a door open** for whoever's reading, two ways:
- *Come learn:* "New to this? Start your first project free at nextwork.ai."
- *Come host:* "Want to run one of these in your city? I'll help you start." (see `start-your-own-build-and-brew.md`)

Pick one door per post. Don't stack CTAs. If the credit already implies the invite, let it.

## Trackable link (so you can see the loop working)
Use one link and tag it, so NextWork can tell which posts actually bring people in:
```
nextwork.ai/?utm_source=buildbrew&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=<yourcity>
```
Confirm the real signup / host link with NextWork before you post (the path here is a placeholder). One
short, tagged link in the caption beats a bare domain.
