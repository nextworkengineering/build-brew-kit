# Examples

## A real worked example
`austin-build-brew/` is a full event run through the story-first workflow: a single post plus a carousel (tonal, no accent), the Instagram PNGs and the LinkedIn PDF, and `caption.md` with the growth loop closed in the host's voice. Its `README.md` shows the story-first trail.

To make your own, hand your agent `AGENT.md` and point it at your photos; it builds the boards on the Paper canvas. `_test-photos/` are real Build & Brew photos you can practice on.

Want to add yours? See `../CONTRIBUTING.md`.
