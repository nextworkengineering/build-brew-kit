# The free Paper path

You can start on a free Paper account. Here's what works and where the wall is.

## The limit (read this first)
Paper's free tier has a **weekly cap on agent (MCP) calls**, and a full carousel build burns through it fast. So on free:

- **Single posts work well.** One photo, one line, a few calls. Do these.
- **Full carousels usually don't fit.** A 6 to 8 slide build will hit the cap partway. Save carousels for a paid account.
- If tools start failing with a usage error mid-build, that's the cap. Close and reopen Paper, or switch to a single post.

## Two ways to stay inside the budget
1. **Single-post first.** It's the recommended format anyway, and one share is a win.
2. **Code-render path.** Skip Paper's cap entirely: have your agent fill an archetype in `templates/archetypes/` and render it once with `render.sh`, instead of many small Paper edits.

## When it's worth upgrading
Paid Paper unlocks the full carousel and, honestly, a much bigger rabbit hole: the same skills build marketing sites, UI mockups, and product visuals. The event post is the on-ramp.
