# Voice

NextWork sounds like a smart friend who's already figured out what you're struggling with. Confident without arrogance, warm without being soft, direct without being cold.

## Four principles
1. **Outcomes, not features.** "Here's what you built" beats "here's our platform."
2. **Specific, not vague.** "45 people, one room, six projects shipped" beats "a great turnout."
3. **Progress, not guilt.** Celebrate what happened. Never nag.
4. **Human, not corporate.** Write like you talk.

## Caption do / don't
| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| come build with us | Sign up now |
| we shipped six projects tonight | An amazing evening of innovation |
| your first project, live | Unlock your potential |
| Austin, round two | Introducing: Build & Brew ATX |

Keep it to one line. Lowercase or sentence case. Let the photo do the talking.

No em dashes or en dashes ("—", "–") in a caption or a line. Use a period, a comma, or "and". A stray em dash is the loudest AI tell, so scan the caption and every board line before you post and strip any.
