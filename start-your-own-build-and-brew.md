# Start your own Build & Brew

You don't need to be an expert. If you can run this kit, you can run a night. Here's the whole thing
on one page. The first one is always smaller and scrappier than you picture, and that's fine.

## What it is
A few people, a room, coffee, a couple of hours. You pair off, each pair builds one real, working
thing, everyone demos at the end. That's the whole format. The magic is that people **build instead
of watch.** Nobody leaves with notes. They leave with something they shipped.

## What you need
- A room. A cafe corner, a coworking space, a friend's living room. It doesn't have to be nice.
- 4 to 20 people. Small is good. Six people who ship beats sixty who sit.
- Laptops, wifi, coffee.
- A couple of build prompts so nobody faces a blank page. NextWork projects work great as starters:
  everyone picks one and builds it live.

## Run of the night (steal this)
- **Doors + coffee.** Let people land and say hi.
- **Pair up.** Match a first-timer with someone a step ahead. Pairs ship more than solo.
- **Build.** One real thing per pair. You float, unblock, and keep the energy up.
- **Demo.** Each pair shows what works. Real code, real nerves. This is the moment people remember.
- **Photos.** Grab the candid ones while it's happening. You'll want them for the post.

## Then post it (that's what this kit is for)
Point your AI agent at your photos and this kit. It walks you through a post that builds *your* name as
someone who ships and gathers people, and leaves a door open for the next person. Tag everyone who came.
Credit what got you here. See `README.md` and `templates/caption-and-tags.md`.

## Why it's worth it
Running one of these is leadership on the record. It grows your name, your network, and your skill at
the same time, and it costs you a couple of hours and some coffee. Here's the part that compounds: the
people you empower tonight are the ones who run the next one. You upskilled, you found the courage to
host, and now you hand that same thing to someone else. That's the whole loop, and you're the start of
it in your city.

## Make it yours
This is a starting point, not a rulebook. The best nights have your fingerprint on them: your city, your
crowd, your weird good idea. That's the magic trick (see `magic_trick.md`), and it's the part no template
can hand you.
